Laboratory and Analytical Skills

Laboratory and analytical experiences complemented field-based learning by introducing structured approaches to data handling, analysis, and interpretation. The laboratory environment emphasized methodological accuracy and caution in producing meaningful data.

Through basic laboratory practice, I developed an understanding of how environmental observations can be translated into data-based interpretation, highlighting inherent limitations and uncertainties.

Exposure to analytical approaches fostered awareness of methodological suitability, data quality, and the contextual interpretation of analytical results in environmental research.
