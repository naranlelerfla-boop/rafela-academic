Why Coastal Ecosystems Are More Fragile Than We Think

Written by Elaa · February 2025 . Bali, Indonesia

Coastal ecosystems represent dynamic interfaces between terrestrial and marine environments. Despite their apparent resilience, these systems are increasingly vulnerable to cumulative pressures.

Climate change intensifies sea-level rise and extreme weather events, while coastal urbanization accelerates habitat degradation. These stressors often operate simultaneously, exceeding ecological thresholds that are not immediately visible.

From an integrated coastal zone management perspective, understanding these hidden vulnerabilities is essential for developing adaptive, science-based policy responses.
