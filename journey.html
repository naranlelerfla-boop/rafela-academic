Explore My Journey

A reflection on my academic journey, shaped by field-based learning, analytical training, and a growing interest in marine and environmental research.

FOUNDATION

Early Interest and Academic Foundation

Living within a small island context offers continuous exposure to coastal and marine systems where ecological processes are closely linked with social and economic structures.

This environment encouraged an academic interest in understanding environmental change through scientific and ecological perspectives.

FIELDWORK

Field Experiences and Practical Exposure

Field-based learning strengthened my understanding of coastal and marine ecosystems through direct engagement in mangrove, seagrass, and plankton-related activities.

These experiences provided practical insight into ecosystem dynamics beyond theoretical frameworks.

LAB & ANALYSIS

Laboratory and Analytical Skills

Laboratory exposure complemented fieldwork by introducing structured approaches to data handling, analysis, and interpretation.

These experiences fostered awareness of methodological accuracy and interdisciplinary analytical perspectives.

DIRECTION

Current Focus and Future Direction

My academic focus has gradually shifted toward identifying early indicators of environmental change through integrative ecological approaches.

This direction reflects a long-term commitment to sustainability- oriented environmental research.
