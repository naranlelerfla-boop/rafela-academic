Current Focus and Future Direction

As my academic and practical experiences have developed, my focus has increasingly shifted toward identifying early indicators of environmental change through integrative ecological approaches.

Rather than relying solely on structural or morphological indicators, growing attention has been drawn to biological and chemical processes that reflect ecosystem stress and adaptive mechanisms.

This trajectory reflects a long-term commitment to evidence-based, sustainability-oriented environmental research.
